CallBrief

Paste a number. Get geography, patterns, and your memory. Nothing leaves this phone.

After a call, open Recents, copy the number, paste here. This app cannot see the ringing line.

Free mode: no live “who owns this” name from paid databases. What you get is area-code geography, toll-free/premium clues, spoof-ish patterns, and the log you build.
